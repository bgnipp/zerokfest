FAQ

Cost

Free, but you contribute — a set, a meal, a role, ingredients. Same as 2k. Getting there is on you: figure a LIH flight, a shared car, and a food chip-in.

Kids?

Yes if they participate. Ocean days need a grown-up who can swim with them. Night swim is optional and called if the water is ugly.

Not a musician?

Even better. Can't-hit-a-wrong-note kit, ocean events, kitchen, glow swim.

Bring

Reef-safe sunscreen, rash guard, headlamp, one instrument, reusable cup, a rain layer for night trades.

Weather

June is better than winter on the North Shore, not a drought. Pack for sun and a squall.

Who is this for?

If you already know 2k or 9k, you know. If you don't: invite-only, limited, raise a hand and we will see.
